More Than a Certificate

Most instructor trainings end when the day does. Becoming a F.I.T Trainer is where it starts.

F.I.T is an official brand partner of EMD UK, the national governing body for group exercise, and the F.I.T Trainer programme is proudly sponsored by Platinum Performing Arts. F.I.T Jam and F.I.T Combat are also taught as CPD instructor training at Platinum Academy of Performing Arts, giving performers a route into the fitness industry. That means training with a brand the industry recognises, backed by people who believe in growing talent.

You leave certified in a concept that has been delivered on stages around the world for over 13 years. You also leave with a mentor, a community, and new doors to walk through.

Choose Your Concept

Both concepts sit under F.I.T, Faye's Intentional Training, built on one idea: Focus On The Feeling. These are classes people remember because of how they made them feel.

F.I.T Jam

High-energy, inclusive dance fitness that blends dance, movement and fitness into a workout people love. Its step-by-step teaching method breaks routines down clearly, so participants build confidence as they move. You'll sharpen your musicality, communication and leadership. You'll also learn the choreography structure, cueing and coaching layers that make a room come alive.

F.I.T Combat

Music-driven, cardio-focused training built on powerful combat-inspired movement. It builds fitness, rhythm and coordination while staying accessible for every level. You'll master the techniques, movement patterns and musical timing. You'll also learn safe practice, energy management and the commanding instructor presence a Combat class needs.

Train in one, or train in both.

What's Included

  • Full-day concept training. A hands-on day learning the F.I.T method, choreography, coaching cues and how to deliver the F.I.T feeling, ending in your certification.
  • Ongoing mentoring support. Faye stays in your corner after the training day, helping you launch and grow your class.
  • Business mentoring. Learn how to set up a successful class: pricing, venues, filling your room and building a loyal community.
  • Access to F.I.T events and coaching containers. Join the F.I.T community at live events and be part of the brand's biggest moments along with improving your business with access to coaching.
  • Presenting opportunities. Get seen. Certified trainers can be considered to teach on the F.I.T App, at events and on retreats.
  • F.I.T merchandise. Represent the brand in official F.I.T kit.
  • Discounted retreats abroad. Exclusive trainer rates on F.I.T retreats, from Mind Fit in Zanzibar to the F.I.T Trip spa break in Bulgaria.

  • Run your own F.I.T class in gyms, health clubs and community settings, with a recognised brand behind it, not just someone else's timetable.
  • Teach on the F.I.T App and reach people far beyond your local studio.

  • Grow a business, not just a schedule, with Faye mentoring you on how to build income beyond the class fee.

FAQs

Do I need to be qualified already?

The F.I.T Trainer programme is designed for qualified group exercise instructors. Not sure if you meet the requirements? Enquire and Faye's team will advise.

How long is the training?

One full, in-person training day, followed by ongoing mentoring support as you launch your class.

Can I train in both F.I.T Jam and F.I.T Combat?

Yes. Many trainers choose both to give their timetable more variety.

What happens after I'm certified?

You can start teaching your F.I.T class, receive ongoing mentoring, and be considered for opportunities on the F.I.T App, at events and on retreats.
