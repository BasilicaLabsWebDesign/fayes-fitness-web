Coaching and Consulting

Faye helps fitness and wellness professionals grow their visibility and income, and elevate their businesses through 1-1 business coaching, group coaching, mentorship and consulting. Her expertise comes from years at the top of the industry: as Group Exercise Manager at the elite Third Space health club, opening a health club, delivering fitness instructor training at Johnson Digital Film Studios, and creating fitness concepts for talent and brands. She also offers specialist retreat consulting and group exercise programme consulting, and you can train to become a qualified F.I.T trainer, teaching her signature concepts. Wherever you are on your journey, there’s a way to grow with Faye.
