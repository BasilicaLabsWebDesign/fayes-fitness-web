Flodesk: saving enquiry emails to your mailing list
This can be done now. It doesn't have to wait for the rest of the email set-up. Here's what it takes.

What I need from you
1. A Flodesk API key. In Flodesk, go to your account settings, then Integrations, then API, and create a key. Please don't paste it into a chat: when I build this I'll give you a secure box to put it in, and it's stored as a hidden setting on Cloudflare, never in the site's files.
2. Which list it should go to. In Flodesk that's a "segment", for example a new one called "Website enquiries". Tell me its name, or I can set one up through the key.
3. A yes to a consent box. UK law (UK GDPR and the PECR rules on marketing emails) means someone who sends an enquiry can only be added to a marketing list if they agree to it. Each form would get an unticked box, "Add me to Faye's mailing list", and only people who tick it are saved. Without that box I'd advise against saving enquiry emails to a mailing list.

How it would work
- Someone fills in the Contact or Work Gallery form, ticks the box and presses Send to Faye.
- The site quietly passes their name and email to Flodesk and adds them to your chosen segment. The visitor never connects to Flodesk directly, and your key stays private.
- The email to you still opens exactly as it does now, so no enquiry is lost if Flodesk is ever slow.
- If you like, Flodesk can send them its own "please confirm" email first (double opt-in), so only real addresses join.

Before the site goes live, the privacy policy will also need to say that mailing list sign-ups are kept in Flodesk.
