Why it works only sometimes on your phone
- A brand-new domain takes a while to settle across the internet. Mobile networks keep their own copies of where each address points, including "this address doesn't exist" if it was looked up before it was ready. They keep those for anything from 30 minutes to a day or two, and different servers on the same network can give different answers. That's why it fails, then works, then works after a refresh.
- The page without styling is the same thing: the page arrived, but the separate request for its styling didn't get through. Your screenshots also show a weak 4G signal, which makes this more likely.
- If the domain was set up today or yesterday, this almost always clears up on its own within 24 to 48 hours, with nothing to do.

What to do
1. Add www (whoever logs in to Cloudflare): Workers & Pages, then fayes-fitness-web, then Settings, then Domains & Routes. Choose Add, then Custom domain, and enter www.fayeedwardsfit.com. Cloudflare sets up the address and its certificate by itself.
2. Quick test: open the link on Wi-Fi, and in Safari as well as Brave. If it works every time on Wi-Fi, it's the mobile network still catching up.
3. If it's still happening after 48 hours, there's one setting to try in Cloudflare for fayeedwardsfit.com: Speed, then Optimization, then Protocol Optimization, and turn off HTTP/3. It's a newer kind of connection that some mobile networks handle badly, and it can cause exactly these failed and half-loaded pages. Tell me afterwards and I'll check again.
