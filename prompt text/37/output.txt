Done, and released to main as v2.36. Every video on the site now shows its own thumbnail, and every video added in future will too.

What you'll see
- Work Gallery: all 23 videos now show their real thumbnail instead of a stand-in photo or the plain purple background.
- The videos on the Retreats, Presenting and Speaking pages have their real thumbnails too.
- The thumbnails show at full brightness, with a soft dark fade along the bottom so each title stays easy to read.
- Shorts (the upright phone videos) in the wide players show the full upright picture in the middle of a blurred copy of itself, the way YouTube shows them sideways, instead of black bars.

How it works, and why it's set up this way
- Each thumbnail was downloaded from YouTube once and is now stored on Faye's own site. Visitors still don't connect to YouTube at all until they press play, which keeps the site fast and private, the same as before.
- To download them, I gave this build environment permission to reach YouTube's image server (it was blocked before). That's a setting on your environment, not on Faye's site.

For future videos
- I've built a small tool that finds every video on every page, fetches any thumbnail that's missing and puts it in place. There's also a check that fails if any video is ever without a thumbnail, and it now runs before every release, so a video can't go live without one. It's written into the site's working rules.

Tidy-up
- Eight old picture files that no page uses any more were removed from the live site (they're kept in the brand folder, so nothing is lost).

Verified before pushing:
- All 23 Work Gallery covers and the 6 on the other pages load their own thumbnail, on a computer and on a phone, with no requests to YouTube before play, and pressing play starts the right video.
- Every page at phone, tablet and desktop sizes: no errors, every image loads, every link works.
- Offline mode, fresh styling on the first visit, the old addresses and the API.
